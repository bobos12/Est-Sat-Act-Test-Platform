Is this the official EST?+

No. 16hundred is an independent practice platform. Our exams are original questions written to match the official EST blueprint: the same topics, question types, number of questions and timing.

Which exams are available?+

EST I Math (50 questions, 75 minutes) and EST I Literacy (85 questions in 3 modules, 90 minutes), as full mocks and shorter topic practice sets. EST II Math Level 1 and 2, Biology and Chemistry are being prepared.

What's free and what needs a subscription?+

Exams marked FREE only need a free account, no card. Pro unlocks every exam in the library. Plans and prices are shown on the pricing page before you pay.

How can I follow my child's progress?+

Every exam your child submits is saved to their dashboard with the score, the time used, a breakdown by topic and an explanation for every question.

How do we pay?+

By card, through Paymob's secure checkout. We never see or store your card details.
